<h1 style="text-align: justify;">Microsoft AZ-900 Exam Preparation Guide: How to Study Effectively</h1>

<p style="text-align: justify;">The Microsoft AZ-900 Exam is a good starting point for anyone who wants to understand Microsoft Azure and build a foundation in cloud computing. However, preparing for it effectively is not just about memorizing Azure service names. You need to understand what different Azure services do, when they should be used, and how cloud concepts connect with real business requirements.</p>

<h2 style="text-align: justify;">Understand the Azure Fundamentals First</h2>

<p style="text-align: justify;">Start your preparation with core cloud concepts. Make sure you understand the difference between public, private, and hybrid cloud models, along with concepts such as Infrastructure as a Service, Platform as a Service, and Software as a Service.</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">You should also understand the benefits of cloud computing, including scalability, availability, reliability, elasticity, and consumption-based pricing. These concepts provide the foundation for understanding the rest of the AZ-900 exam.</p>

	</li>
	<li>
	<p style="text-align: justify;">Azure storage services</p>

	<li>
	<p style="text-align: justify;">Azure virtual networking</p>

<h2 style="text-align: justify;">Pay Attention to Security and Identity</h2>

<p style="text-align: justify;">Azure security is another important part of the exam. Your preparation should include Microsoft Entra ID, authentication, authorization, multifactor authentication, role-based access control, and the shared responsibility model.</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">Try to understand the difference between identity management and resource access. Scenario-based questions may describe a user, application, or administrator and ask which Azure capability should be used to control access. Also review Azure security and governance services at a high level so you can recognize their purpose when presented with a business scenario.</p>
